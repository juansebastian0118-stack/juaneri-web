SplitLuka$ Support

Something not working, or have a question? Email us at support.splitlukas@juaneri.com and we'll reply within 1–2 business days. If you can, include your account email and a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

How do I invite my friends to a group?

Open the group and tap "Invite friends". With "Invite via WhatsApp" your friends open the link and join the group directly. They can also scan the group's QR code or type the 6-letter code in "Join with code". If you had already added them by name, they can pick their name to keep their expenses.

Does SplitLuka$ charge or move my money?

No. SplitLuka$ works out who owes whom. You pay with the app you already use (for example Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, Pix, Bre-B, Nequi or cash) and then record the payment in SplitLuka$. The person who receives the money confirms it and balances update for everyone.

Can I use SplitLuka$ on iPhone, Android and the web with the same account?

Yes. Your account and groups are the same on iPhone, Android and splitlukas.expo.app. People with different phones can be in the same group and see changes instantly.

What does the free plan include?

Unlimited groups, up to 6 expenses per day (based on your country's time) and up to 100 expenses per year (the allowance resets on your account anniversary). You can always see balances and record payments.

How does Premium work?

Premium is personal: with your plan you add unlimited expenses and scan receipts with AI (30 per month). Prices are in your country's currency. There are monthly, quarterly, semiannual and annual plans that renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple or Google Play account. With the Trip Pass for 10 people, you and 9 other people (chosen by their email) get Premium for one month. The free trial lasts 14 days, once per person; when it ends, Premium Monthly is charged unless you cancel before.

What is sponsoring?

If someone pays for other people in the group (for example, a parent for their kids), they can sponsor them: their sponsored people's expenses are added to their own account. The admin, the co-admin or the sponsor can set it up from the group's member list.

How do I end a trip or close the accounts?

In the group, the admin or co-admin taps "End trip and settle up" (in home groups: "Close month"). Everyone gets what they owe and sees the summary. If you are another member, you can remind the admin; if they don't respond within 48 hours, anyone can close it. When everyone is settled, the group moves to "Past groups".

What is a co-admin?

In groups of 5 or more people, the admin can name a co-admin who has the app. They have the same permissions to manage the group (expenses, members, group name, type and emoji), except naming another co-admin or transferring the admin role.

How do I change my name?

Go to Account → Your name → Change. You can choose whether it also changes in your groups.

Who sees my payment details (Zelle, Venmo, Pix…)?

Only the people in your groups, and only if you agree to share them in Account. They help people pay you quickly; SplitLuka$ doesn't use them for anything else.

Can I delete a group?

Yes, once all of the group's accounts are settled. Deleting it removes its expenses and payments for all members.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app, go to Account → Delete my account. If you can't sign in, email us from your account's email address at support.splitlukas@juaneri.com and we'll delete it within 30 days. More details in the privacy policy (in Spanish).
