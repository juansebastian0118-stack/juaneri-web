SplitLuka$ Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

SplitLuka$ is an app by Juaneri, the sole proprietorship of Juan Sebastián Falla, based in Colombia ("Juaneri" or "we"). This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for and what your rights are, in accordance with Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013.

This is a translation of the Spanish version. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Data controller

Juan Sebastián Falla (Juaneri). Contact email: support.splitlukas@juaneri.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name and email address. Your password is stored encrypted; we cannot see it.
  • Data you enter in the app: names of your groups, names of the people you add to a group, the description and amount of expenses, how they are split, and the payments recorded between members.
  • Trip Pass: if you buy the pass, the emails of the people who will get Premium with your pass. We use them only to activate Premium for them; before entering them, make sure those people agree.
  • Country and language: the country where you live and the language you choose, to show you your currency, your local fast-payment options and your country's time.
  • Details to receive payments (optional): the fast-payment details for your country that you choose to enter, for example your Bre-B key, your Nequi phone number, your Pix key, your Yape phone number or your Zelle email. When you add them to your profile we ask whether you want to share them: if you agree, only the people in your groups can see them, so they know where to send you money when settling up. You can stop sharing them at any time. SplitLuka$ does not use this data for any other purpose. You can change this choice or delete them from Account whenever you want.
  • Receipt photos (Premium only, if you use scanning): the photo you choose is sent to our AI provider to read the merchant, the total and the items. We do not keep the photo: the app only keeps what you decide to save as an expense, plus a record of how many scans you used in the month.
  • Your plan: the plan you have, its start and end dates and whether it renews automatically.
  • Minimal technical data: what is needed to keep you signed in and protect the service (for example, server access logs).

SplitLuka$ does not move money and does not store card details or passwords for your bank accounts or wallets. When you pay with your payment app (Bre-B, Nequi, Pix, Yape, Zelle or another), you make the payment yourself in that app.

3. What we use your data for

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To show the members of each group that group's expenses, balances and payments.
  • To apply the free plan's limits and manage Premium plans.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads based on your profile and we do not use advertising tracking tools.

4. Who can see your information

A group's expenses and payments are visible only to that group's members. Your name is visible to the people you share groups with. No one else has access to your groups.

5. Providers that help us

  • Supabase hosts the database and accounts, on servers located in the United States.
  • Expo (EAS) hosts the web version and distributes the app.
  • Anthropic uses artificial intelligence to read the receipt photos you choose to scan, on servers located in the United States. Under its commercial terms, by default it does not use that data to train its models.
  • Apple and Google process Premium payments; we do not receive your payment details.
  • RevenueCat confirms to us which Premium subscription you have active, using an identifier of your account.
  • Google Workspace sends the app's emails (account confirmation, password recovery and settle-up summaries) and receives support messages.

By using SplitLuka$ you authorize the transfer of your data to these providers, which have adequate security measures.

6. How long we keep data

While your account is active. If you delete a group, its expenses and payments are deleted for all its members. You can delete your account at any time from the app, in Account → Delete my account: your account, your email, your usage record and the groups where you are the only member are deleted immediately. In groups you share with other people, your name is kept next to the recorded expenses and payments so the others' accounts still add up. If you already used the free trial, we keep only an encrypted fingerprint of your email (not the email) so the trial cannot be repeated. You can also request deletion by writing to us; in that case we do it within a maximum of 30 days, except for what the law requires us to keep.

7. Your rights

You can access, update, correct and request deletion of your data, revoke your authorization and file complaints with Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC). To exercise them, write to support.splitlukas@juaneri.com from your account's email address. We answer inquiries within a maximum of 10 business days and claims within a maximum of 15 business days.

8. Minors

SplitLuka$ is not intended for children under 14. If you are under 18, use it with the permission of your parent or guardian.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, encrypted passwords and access rules in the database so each person sees only what concerns them. No system is infallible; if we detect an incident that affects you, we will let you know.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its date. If the change is significant, we will notify you in the app or by email.
