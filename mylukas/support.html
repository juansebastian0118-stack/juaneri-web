MyLuka$ Support

Something not working or have a question? Write to support.mylukas@juaneri.com and we will reply within 1 or 2 business days. If you can, include your account email and a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

How do I mark a payment or income as done?

In My Cash-Flow, tap the circle in the “Done” column of that row. It turns green and the row dims so you can tell done from pending. Tap it again to undo.

How do I edit or delete an item?

Tap the row to open its details: change the name, pay day, amount and category, attach a payment receipt or delete it. On iPhone and Android you can also swipe the row to the left. After deleting you have a few seconds to tap “Undo”.

What are sporadic items?

Income or expenses that are not monthly: yearly, half-yearly or one-off. For example property tax, vehicle tax, insurance, vacations, Christmas gifts or an annual bonus. Add them once in My Sporadic Items with their date and MyLuka$ adds them to the right month.

Do I have to enter everything again each month?

No. Each new month copies the previous one (without sporadic items) and you can always edit it. You can also use “Copy previous month” from the ⋯ menu next to the month name.

How is the savings rate calculated?

It is what you record as an expense in the “Saving & investing” category, divided by your income for the month. Add your monthly savings as an expense in that category so it counts.

When do reminders arrive?

At 8:00 a.m. (your local time) on each payment or income day, at 8:00 p.m. if it is still pending, and on the day of your biggest income we suggest setting money aside for next month’s sporadic expenses. You also see them under the bell in the app.

How do I share my cash flow with my household?

In Profile → My connected household, enter the other person’s email. With the Household plan up to 5 people can see each other’s cash flow.

Can I use MyLuka$ on iPhone, Android and the web with the same account?

Yes. Your account and data are the same on iPhone and at mylukas.expo.app. The Android version is coming soon.

How do the plans work?

There are Personal and Household plans, monthly or yearly, priced in your country’s currency, with a free trial to start. They renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple or Google Play account.

Does MyLuka$ move my money or connect to my bank?

No. MyLuka$ does not connect to your bank or make payments: you record your income and expenses and mark them when done. The analysis is financial education, not advice.

Can I download my data?

Yes. In Profile → Download to Excel choose one month or all your data (one tab per month and one per year of sporadic items).

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. If you can’t sign in, email support.mylukas@juaneri.com from your account email and we will delete it within 30 days. More in the privacy policy.
